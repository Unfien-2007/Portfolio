Real-time disaster monitoring and preparedness for the Philippines.

Safety Guidelines

Before

Prepare an emergency kit (Go-Bag). Know your evacuation routes. Charge electronics.

During

Stay calm. Listen to battery-operated radios. Stay away from windows and coastal areas.

After

Wait for official advice before returning home. Avoid downed power lines.
